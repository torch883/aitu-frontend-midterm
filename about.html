About the University

Our History

Astana IT University was founded in 2019 in Astana, the capital of Kazakhstan. It was created to train specialists in information technology for the digital economy of the country.

The university is located in the building of the EXPO 2017 business center. All lectures, laboratories, the library and the sports arena are under one roof.

Our Mission

To give students modern IT education in English and to prepare them for work in technology companies, in science and in their own start-ups.

What Students Get

  • bachelor programs taught in English
  • practice on real projects with partner companies
  • a library and coworking zones open for self-study
  • student clubs and sport sections

How to Apply

  1. Choose a program on the Programs page.
  2. Pass the Unified National Testing.
  3. Prepare an English language certificate.
  4. Send your documents to the admission office.
